Everything You Need to Know

Are all Kolte-Patil projects registered under RERA?
Yes, 100% of Kolte-Patil developments are registered under Maharashtra RERA or Karnataka RERA. All official RERA numbers are prominently displayed on every project listing.
How can I schedule a cab for a physical site visit?
We offer complimentary pick-and-drop AC cab services across Pune and Bengaluru for site visits. Fill out the enquiry form or click 'Schedule Visit' to request a cab.
Which banks offer pre-approved home loans for Kolte-Patil projects?
