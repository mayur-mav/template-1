Privacy Policy

KOLTE-PATIL PROPERTY ENQUIRY PAGE · PUBLISHED BY M&A VENTURES, AUTHORISED CHANNEL PARTNER

This page explains how M&A Ventures handles information you share through enquiry forms, call buttons and WhatsApp links on this property information page. This page is operated by an Authorised Channel Partner and is not the developer’s official website.

What we collect

  • Your name, mobile number and, if you choose to give it, your email address.
  • The configuration you are interested in and which button or form you used to reach us.
  • Campaign attribution data attached to your visit, such as UTM parameters and advertising click identifiers, along with the page URL and the time of your enquiry.
  • Standard usage data collected by analytics tools, such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location derived from your IP address.

Why we collect it

  • To call you back, send you the price list, floor plans and project documents you asked for, and to arrange a site visit or virtual tour.
  • To keep a record of your enquiry in our customer relationship management systems so a sales advisor can follow up.
  • To measure which campaigns bring genuine enquiries, so our advertising is spent sensibly.

Who receives it

Your enquiry is stored with M&A Ventures and its authorised sales team. It may be shared with the developer or its appointed sales office for the purpose of your enquiry about properties offered by the developer. We use third-party services to store enquiries, send messages and run analytics and advertising, and these providers process the data on our behalf. We do not sell your personal information.

Consent to be contacted

By submitting a form you authorise M&A Ventures and its representatives to contact you about this project by call, SMS, email and WhatsApp, including where your number is registered under DND or NDNC. You can withdraw this consent at any time by writing to the email address below.

Cookies and tracking

The page stores attribution parameters in a first-party cookie for up to 90 days so that a return visit is credited to the campaign you originally came from. Analytics and advertising tags may set their own cookies. You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings; the enquiry forms will still work.

Retention and your rights

We keep enquiry records for as long as needed to follow up on your interest and to meet our legal and accounting obligations. You may ask us to correct or delete your details, or to stop contacting you, by emailing info@markanthony.in

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The version published on this page applies to enquiries made through it.

For privacy questions, contact M&A Ventures using the phone or WhatsApp details shown on the property page.
